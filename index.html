FAQ

Questions investors ask first.

How is this different from eBay or Sortly?
eBay handles the transaction. Sortly handles business inventory. AssetLoop is the only platform that lives in the gap between purchase and resale — telling consumers what they own, what it's worth, and when to sell, before the transaction even happens.
What's the real moat once others enter?
Owned-asset data. Every user makes our pricing model more accurate. Within 18 months of meaningful adoption, we have a dataset no fast-follower can replicate — that's the flywheel.
How do you make money before the SaaS tier launches?
Transaction fees from day one — 2–3% per sale via the platform, versus eBay's 13%. Premium subscription launches in 2027. B2B Data SaaS in 2028. Three revenue streams, all built on the same ownership data layer.
Why hasn't Amazon or Apple already built this?
Conflict of interest. Amazon wants you to buy new. Apple wants you to trade in within their ecosystem. AssetLoop is platform-agnostic — we serve the owner, not a marketplace. That neutrality is the product.
What's your MVP timeline?
Beta launch in 2026. Closed-list testing Q1, public Beta Q3. Premium tier 2027. B2B 2028. The infrastructure converges 2026-2030: AI image recognition, IoT penetration, hyper-personalized AI — we're building right at the inflection point.
